Tester Terms
of Agreement

Please read these terms before joining the Five Goons at Jølle testing team. They explain what is expected of testers and how access to testing is managed.

By joining the FNAJ testing team, you agree to follow the terms below. They help keep testing respectful, useful, and safe for everyone involved.

1. Age Requirement

All testers must be at least 13 years old. Testers must also follow the age requirements and rules of the platforms used for testing and communication.

2. Official Discord Server

All testers are required to be members of the official FNAJ Discord server. Important announcements, testing information, and communication with the development team will take place there. Testers must follow the server rules. Leaving the server means you no longer meet the requirements for the testing team and may result in removal of your tester role.

Join the official FNAJ Discord →

3. Activity and Participation

Testing the game at least once per week is appreciated, but it is not required.

However, testers who remain inactive for an extended period may have their tester role removed without warning. The development team is not required to provide a warning, ask for an explanation, or contact the tester before removing the role.

Testers are encouraged to participate when they can. Personal responsibilities, including school and other commitments, should come first.

4. Respectful Behaviour

All testers must treat developers and other testers respectfully. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and deliberately starting arguments or drama are not allowed.

Constructive criticism is welcome. You may disagree with decisions or dislike features, but express your opinions respectfully. Criticize the game, not the person who created it.

5. Bug Reports and Feedback

Testers are expected to provide honest feedback and report bugs they discover. When possible, include what happened, steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected to happen, and screenshots or recordings if useful.

Do not intentionally submit fake bug reports or claim to have tested features you have not tested. Suggestions are welcome, but there is no guarantee they will be added. Final decisions about features and changes belong to the development team.

6. Confidentiality and Leaks

Private test builds and unreleased content must remain within the authorized testing team unless the development team gives permission to share them.

  • Do not distribute private game builds without permission.
  • Do not leak unreleased content, screenshots, recordings, or story details from private testing.
  • Do not share private testing information with unauthorized people.
  • Do not reupload or redistribute test files without permission.
  • Do not present experimental features as confirmed final content.

If you are unsure whether something can be shared publicly, ask a developer first.

7. Communication

Testers must communicate respectfully and follow the rules of the official Discord server. Spam, impersonating developers, falsely claiming to represent FNAJ, or speaking officially on behalf of the project without permission are not allowed.

Use the appropriate channels for testing discussions and bug reports. If a problem arises between team members, contact a developer rather than deliberately escalating the situation.

8. Use of Test Builds

Test builds are provided for testing purposes. Use them responsibly and follow any additional instructions for a particular build or testing session.

Do not deliberately sabotage testing, exploit bugs to disrupt other testers, or interfere with development. Test builds may contain bugs, unfinished features, or content that changes before release.

9. Rule Violations

Breaking these terms may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or removal from the testing team, depending on the severity of the situation.

Serious violations, including harassment, deliberate sabotage, or leaking private builds, may result in immediate removal without warning. Inactivity is handled separately under Section 3: extended inactivity may result in removal of your tester role without warning.

The development team makes the final decision regarding access to the testing team.

10. Leaving the Testing Team

Testers may leave the testing team whenever they wish. Informing the development team is appreciated, but nobody is required to continue participating if they no longer want to.

Leaving the team does not give permission to share private builds or confidential information obtained during testing.

11. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as FNAJ develops. Testers are expected to follow the current version and any additional testing rules communicated by the development team. Important changes should be announced through the official Discord server.

12. Sharing Tester Access

Tester access is personal and must not be shared. Do not lend your account, tester role, private build access, or testing invitations to anyone who has not been approved by the development team. Each tester is responsible for keeping their own account secure.

13. No Guarantee of Permanent Membership

Being accepted as a tester does not guarantee permanent membership. The development team may change the testing team or remove access when project needs change or when a tester no longer meets the requirements.

14. No False Representation

Testers must not claim to be developers, official representatives, or decision-makers for FNAJ unless they have been explicitly given that permission. Do not make announcements or promises on behalf of the project.

15. Unapproved Modifications

Do not modify, redistribute, or create and share unofficial versions of private test builds without permission from the development team. Personal testing experiments must not be presented as official versions of FNAJ.

16. Reporting Security Issues

If you discover an issue that could expose private information, compromise accounts, or allow unauthorized access to test builds, report it privately to the development team as soon as possible. Do not exploit it or share details publicly.

17. No Guaranteed Rewards

Being a tester does not automatically guarantee payment, rewards, game credits, or special privileges. Any reward or benefit must be explicitly confirmed by the development team.

18. Developer Decisions

Testers are welcome to suggest changes and respectfully disagree, but the development team has the final say on the game's features, design, development, and testing process. Being a tester does not give anyone the right to demand that a suggestion is implemented.

19. Voluntary Participation

Testing is voluntary. Do not pressure other testers to play, provide feedback, or take part in activities. Weekly testing is appreciated but not required, as explained in Section 3.

20. Story Spoilers

Do not publicly reveal major story events, character information, surprises, or other unreleased story details learned from private test builds. Only share spoilers when the development team has approved the content for public release.

21. Agreement and Acceptance

By joining and participating in the FNAJ testing team, you confirm that you meet the minimum age requirement, have joined the official Discord server, have read and understood these terms, and agree to follow them.

You understand that breaking these terms may result in losing your tester role, and that extended inactivity may result in removal without warning.

If you do not agree with these terms, you should not join or continue participating in the FNAJ testing team.
