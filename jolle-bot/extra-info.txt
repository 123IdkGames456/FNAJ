JØLLE
Jølle is a very strong human character in the project.
His full face is not shown.
The camera system is designed to hide his movement.
His voice is around 15 years old.

TIMMY WILLIAM
Timmy William created tapes, cameras and notes as part of the story.
His story continues through the game's tapes and notes.
Timmy dies in the Night 5 tape.

GAMEPLAY
The game is planned around five nights running from 12 AM to 6 AM.
The player uses cameras, doors and a lure system.
Jølle can move between rooms and can eventually pursue the player.
